    Learning Shapes for the use of Web Design!

    Image created by me on Adobe Photoshop.

    Shapes play a big role in web design because they can influence how people feel and interact with a website. Different shapes can communicate different emotions and ideas. For example, circles often represent unity, comfort, and friendliness, while squares and rectangles can create a sense of stability, structure, and professionalism. Triangles can represent movement, direction, or energy, making them useful for drawing attention to specific areas of a webpage. Designers use these shapes in logos, buttons, backgrounds, and other visual elements to help communicate a brand's message and create a certain mood for the audience. Shapes can also affect how users navigate a website and how easily they understand its layout. Rounded buttons may feel more welcoming and approachable, while sharp edges and geometric patterns can create a modern, bold appearance. Using shapes consistently throughout a webpage can help organize information, guide the viewer's attention, and make the overall design more visually appealing. When shapes are combined thoughtfully with colors, typography, and spacing, they can improve the user experience and make a website feel more professional, memorable, and engaging.
